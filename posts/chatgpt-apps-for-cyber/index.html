ChatGPT Apps You Should Use to Stay Safe (2026 Guide)

📅 Published March 26, 2026 ·ai-toolsdefensive-securityosint

Turn ChatGPT into a cybersecurity assistant, OSINT toolkit, and scam shield using built-in apps.

⚠️ Why This Matters

The internet in 2026 is flooded with:

  • AI-generated phishing pages
  • Deepfake scam calls
  • Malicious shortened URLs
  • Fake marketplaces & listings

Most people still click first and think later.

You don’t.

You verify first.

🛡️ 1. Malwarebytes App (Link Intelligence Engine)

🔗 https://chatgpt.com/apps/malwarebytes/asdk_app_6943d1778c8c81918f4c9ddf4849ca41

🧠 What It Does

Protect yourself from phishing and scams by verifying links, domains, emails, and phone numbers before you click, call, or reply.

Get instant reputation insights, ownership details, and risk levels for:

  • Unknown numbers
  • Suspicious emails
  • Shortened URLs
  • Unfamiliar links

⚡ Why It Matters

This is basically your real-time OSINT scanner inside ChatGPT.

Instead of guessing if a link is safe → you interrogate it.

🧠 2. Norton App (AI Scam Analyst)

🔗 https://chatgpt.com/apps/norton/asdk_app_699dc623e5488191955863d8c4a7ad19

🧠 What It Does

Stay protected while you chat.

Norton brings real-time, AI-powered scam detection directly into your ChatGPT experience, backed by the Norton Genie AI assistant.

You can:

  • Share suspicious emails, texts, or messages
  • Analyze images & phishing screenshots
  • Check links before opening
  • Ask security/privacy questions

⚡ Why It Matters

This is like having a SOC analyst in your pocket.

Instead of reacting after getting hacked → you prevent the attack.

🎨 3. Figma App (Visual OSINT & Thinking Tool)

🔗 https://chatgpt.com/apps/figma/connector_68df038e0ba48191908c8434991bbac2

🧠 What It Does

The Figma app in ChatGPT generates:

  • Diagrams
  • Flowcharts
  • Presentations
  • Social media assets
  • Investigation maps

You can turn ideas into:

  • Link analysis graphs
  • OSINT investigation boards
  • Learning diagrams

⚡ Why It Matters

This is underrated for hackers & investigators.

Use it to:

  • Connect dots in investigations
  • Map attacker infrastructure
  • Visualize phishing campaigns
  • Learn complex concepts visually
